# Unit 1 — Introduction to Data Analytics

## What is Data Analysis?

Data analysis is choosing the right approach to answer a question with data.

## Key Idea

A data analyst should start with the business question, not the visualization.

Question → Analytical Approach → Data → Analysis → Insight → Decision

## Four Main Questions

| Business Question | Analytics Type |
|---|---|
| What happened? | Descriptive Analytics |
| Why did it happen? | Diagnostic Analytics |
| What will happen? | Predictive Analytics |
| What should we do? | Prescriptive Analytics |

## Data vs Insight

Data:
Sales = ₹50 Crore

Information:
Sales were ₹50 Crore this year, same as last year.

Insight:
Overall sales were flat because growth in one channel was offset by decline in another.

Decision:
Increase investment in the stronger channel and investigate the weaker channel.

## Analyst Mindset

❌ What chart should I make?

✅ What business question am I trying to answer?

Then determine:
1. What data is required?
2. What analysis is appropriate?
3. What insight does the data provide?
4. How should the insight be communicated?
5. What decision can the business make?

## Key Takeaway

A good data analyst does not just provide data.
They use the right analytical approach to answer the right business question.

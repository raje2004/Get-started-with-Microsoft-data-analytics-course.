# Unit 2 — Types of Data Analytics

## 1. Descriptive Analytics

### Question:
What happened?

### Purpose:
Quantifies and summarizes historical/current data.

### Examples:
- Total sales
- Sales by region
- Sales by product
- Monthly sales trend
- Revenue by channel

### Key word:
WHAT?

---

## 2. Diagnostic Analytics

### Question:
Why did it happen?

### Purpose:
Find the causes, drivers, and relationships behind an outcome.

### Process:
1. Identify what to investigate
2. Collect related data
3. Find patterns/drivers

### Example:
Sales decreased because a region underperformed and heavy discounting affected margins.

### Key word:
WHY?

---

## 3. Predictive Analytics

### Question:
What is likely to happen next?

### Purpose:
Uses historical patterns to estimate future outcomes.

### Examples:
- Sales forecast
- Revenue forecast
- Demand prediction
- Customer churn prediction

### Key word:
WHAT NEXT?

---

## 4. Prescriptive Analytics

### Question:
What should we do?

### Purpose:
Evaluate possible actions and their likely outcomes to support decisions.

### Examples:
- Pricing optimization
- Discount strategy
- Resource allocation
- Scenario analysis

### Key word:
WHAT SHOULD WE DO?

---

## 5. Cognitive Analytics

### Question:
What can AI help us understand?

### Purpose:
Uses AI to process complex or large-scale data, especially
unstructured data.

### Examples:
- Customer review analysis
- Sentiment analysis
- Support ticket analysis
- Social media analysis
- Pattern discovery

### Key word:
AI / UNDERSTAND

---

# Analytics Journey

What happened?
        ↓
Descriptive
        ↓
Why did it happen?
        ↓
Diagnostic
        ↓
What is likely to happen?
        ↓
Predictive
        ↓
What should we do?
        ↓
Prescriptive
        ↓
Business Decision

AI/Cognitive analytics can support the analytical process.

---

# Important Principle

Different business questions require different analytical approaches.

Do not choose an analytical method just because the visualization looks good.

Start with:
Business Question → Analytics Type → Data → Analysis → Insight → Decision

---

# AI in Analytics

AI can:
- Generate summaries
- Suggest factors to investigate
- Detect anomalies
- Analyze unstructured data
- Translate natural-language questions into queries

The analyst must still:
- Validate the output
- Check data quality
- Confirm the correct analytics approach
- Decide whether the result is trustworthy enough to act on
